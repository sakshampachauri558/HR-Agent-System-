# Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope
1.1 This policy sets out the types of leave available to all confirmed, full-time employees of the Company at its India offices, the entitlement and accrual rules for each leave type, and the process for applying for leave.
1.2 Interns, employees on probation, and employees on a fixed-term contract of less than six months are covered by a separate leave schedule communicated at the time of offer and are not covered by this policy.
1.3 The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## 2. Types of Leave

### 2.1 Casual Leave
2.1.1 Every confirmed employee is entitled to 12 casual leave days per calendar year, credited in full at the start of the leave year.
2.1.2 Casual leave is intended for short personal matters and may be taken for a maximum of 3 consecutive working days at a time without being reclassified as earned leave.
2.1.3 Casual leave must be applied for at least 1 working day in advance, except in a genuine emergency, in which case the employee must inform their manager on the same day and regularise the application within 3 working days of returning to work.

### 2.2 Sick Leave
2.2.1 Every confirmed employee is entitled to 8 sick leave days per calendar year, credited in full at the start of the leave year.
2.2.2 Sick leave does not carry forward to the next calendar year under any circumstances and is not encashable.
2.2.3 Sick leave taken for more than 2 consecutive working days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 5 working days of returning to work. Sick leave availed without the required medical certificate will be converted to leave without pay.

### 2.3 Earned Leave (Privilege Leave)
2.3.1 Every confirmed employee accrues earned leave at the rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service, amounting to 18 days per calendar year for an employee who works the full year.
2.3.2 Earned leave must ordinarily be applied for at least 5 working days in advance and is subject to manager approval based on business and team coverage needs.
2.3.3 A minimum of 5 consecutive earned leave days must be taken at least once every calendar year; this block-leave requirement supports fraud-prevention controls in finance and other sensitive roles.

## 3. Carry-Forward Rules

### 3.1 Casual Leave Carryover
3.1.1 Up to 5 unused casual leave days may carry forward into the next calendar year with manager approval.
3.1.2 Carried-over casual leave days must be used within the first quarter of the following year or they are forfeited. No encashment of carried-over casual leave is permitted.
3.1.3 Casual leave in excess of 5 unused days at year-end lapses automatically and is not paid out.

### 3.2 Earned Leave Carryover and Encashment
3.2.1 Up to 15 unused earned leave days may be carried forward into the next calendar year.
3.2.2 Earned leave accumulated beyond 15 days at year-end, up to a maximum of 10 additional days, is automatically encashed at the employee's current basic salary rate and paid out with the January payroll.
3.2.3 An employee may voluntarily encash up to 10 earned leave days in a calendar year while still employed, subject to a minimum balance of 10 earned leave days remaining after encashment.
3.2.4 On resignation or termination, all unused earned leave (with no upper cap) is encashed and paid out as part of full and final settlement. Unused casual and sick leave are not paid out on exit.

## 4. Public Holidays
4.1 The Company observes 10 public holidays each calendar year, published on the HR portal by 15 December of the preceding year.
4.2 Employees required to work on a published public holiday are entitled to a compensatory day off within 30 days, in addition to applicable holiday pay under local law.

## 5. Maternity and Paternity Leave
5.1 Maternity leave is granted for 26 weeks in accordance with the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, for the first two children, and 12 weeks for the third child onward.
5.2 Paternity leave of 15 calendar days is granted to male and non-birthing parent employees, to be availed within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.
5.3 Maternity and paternity leave are in addition to, and do not draw down, casual, sick, or earned leave balances.

## 6. Leave Application Process
6.1 All leave must be applied for through the HR portal. Verbal or email-only leave requests are not valid unless the HR portal is unavailable, in which case the employee must regularise the request on the portal within 2 working days.
6.2 Leave is deemed approved only on explicit manager sign-off in the portal; an unanswered request is not automatically approved.
6.3 Leave balances are visible to employees on the HR portal at all times and are reconciled by the HR team on a monthly basis.

## 7. Unauthorized Absence
7.1 Any absence not covered by an approved leave application, and not otherwise regularised within 3 working days, is treated as unauthorized absence.
7.2 Unauthorized absence exceeding 5 consecutive working days without communication may be treated as voluntary abandonment of employment, subject to the Company's disciplinary process.
7.3 Any day marked as unauthorized absence is treated as leave without pay for payroll purposes.
