# Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope
1.1 This policy describes which business expenses the Company reimburses, the documentation required, submission timelines, and approval authority. It applies to all confirmed employees.
1.2 Expenses must be reasonable, business-related, and incurred in line with the traveller's approved grade and the category limits in this policy. The Company does not reimburse expenses that are personal in nature or that could reasonably have been avoided.

## 2. Categories of Reimbursable Expenses
2.1 The following categories are reimbursable, subject to the specific rules in Sections 4 through 6: business travel, local conveyance, internet and mobile connectivity for approved work-from-home employees, professional development and certification, and client entertainment.
2.2 Expenses outside these categories require prior written approval from the employee's functional head and Finance before being incurred.

## 3. Submission Process and Timelines

### 3.1 How to Submit
3.1.1 All reimbursement claims must be submitted through the Expense Portal with scanned original invoices attached. Claims without a valid, itemised invoice will not be processed.
3.1.2 A claim must clearly state the business purpose, date, category, and amount for each line item.

### 3.2 Timelines
3.2.1 Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days require additional written justification and Finance Head approval, and claims submitted more than 90 days after the expense will not be reimbursed except in extraordinary circumstances.
3.2.2 Approved claims are paid out with the next payroll cycle following approval, and in any case within 15 working days of approval.

## 4. Travel Reimbursement
4.1 Domestic travel is to be booked through the Company's approved travel desk wherever possible. Economy class air travel is reimbursed for journeys under 5 hours; employees at Director level and above may book business class for journeys over 5 hours.
4.2 A per-diem of ₹2,500 per day is payable for domestic travel to cover meals and incidentals, and does not require individual meal receipts.
4.3 International travel per-diem rates vary by destination city and are published separately on the HR portal; all international travel requires pre-trip approval from the functional head and Finance.
4.4 Local conveyance (cabs, auto-rickshaws, ride-hailing apps) for business purposes is reimbursed against the trip receipt or a screenshot of the fare from the ride-hailing app.

## 5. Internet and Mobile Reimbursement
5.1 Employees approved to work from home 2 or more days per week under the Remote Work Policy are eligible for a monthly reimbursement of ₹1,000 towards home internet and mobile connectivity, on submission of the relevant bill.
5.2 This reimbursement is processed automatically each month once an internet or mobile bill is uploaded to the Expense Portal and does not require a fresh manager approval each month.

## 6. Professional Development and Certification Reimbursement
6.1 Employees may claim up to ₹50,000 per calendar year towards job-related courses, conferences, and industry certifications, subject to prior approval from their manager confirming relevance to the employee's role.
6.2 Certification reimbursements above ₹20,000 in a calendar year are subject to a 1-year service retention bond: an employee who resigns within 12 months of receiving such reimbursement must repay it on a pro-rated basis for the unserved period.
6.3 Books, subscriptions, and online-learning platform licenses directly relevant to the employee's role may be claimed under this category, within the overall ₹50,000 annual limit.

## 7. Client Entertainment
7.1 Client entertainment expenses (meals, small gifts of nominal value) require prior manager approval and must name the client and business purpose on the claim. A single client entertainment claim may not exceed ₹8,000 without Finance Head approval.

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
8.1 The following are never reimbursable under this policy: traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment, alcohol (except where explicitly approved as part of a client entertainment budget under Section 7), airport lounge access on domestic travel, and any expense lacking a valid invoice.

## 9. Documentation Requirements
9.1 All claims above ₹5,000 require a GST-compliant invoice showing the vendor's GSTIN wherever the vendor is GST-registered. Claims without a compliant invoice may be reimbursed as a taxable perquisite instead, at Finance's discretion.

## 10. Approval Authority and Escalation
10.1 Claims up to ₹25,000 may be approved by the employee's direct manager. Claims above ₹25,000 require Finance Head approval in addition to manager approval.
10.2 An employee dissatisfied with a rejected claim may escalate in writing to the Finance Head within 15 working days of rejection.
