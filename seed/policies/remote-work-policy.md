# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose
1.1 This policy describes when and how employees may work away from a Company office, the equipment and connectivity support provided, and the expectations for availability, security, and performance while working remotely.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Who May Work Remotely
2.1.1 Remote work under this policy is available to confirmed employees whose role has been designated "remote-eligible" by their functional head, based on the nature of the work and client or team coverage needs.
2.1.2 Employees on probation are not eligible for scheduled remote work and are expected to work from a Company office for the duration of their probation period, except for a single-day exception approved in advance by their manager.
2.1.3 Eligibility is reviewed at each performance cycle and may be revoked by a functional head if attendance, availability, or output does not meet expectations.

### 2.2 Hybrid Work-From-Home Days
2.2.1 Employees in remote-eligible roles may work from home for up to 2 days per week, to be agreed with their manager in advance and recorded on the HR portal.
2.2.2 Managers may temporarily increase the weekly work-from-home allowance to 5 days for a defined period (for example, during a medical recovery or a family emergency) with HR's concurrence.
2.2.3 Client-facing teams must ensure at least one team member is present in the office, or reachable on video, during core client hours regardless of individual work-from-home schedules.

### 2.3 Fully Remote Roles
2.3.1 A small number of roles may be designated fully remote at the time of hiring, subject to written approval from the relevant Vice President and HR. Fully remote employees must still attend quarterly in-person team meetings at Company expense.

## 3. Home Office Support

### 3.1 Equipment Stipend
3.1.1 Employees approved for regular work-from-home (2 or more days per week) receive a one-time home office equipment stipend of ₹15,000, reimbursable against invoices for a desk, chair, monitor, or similar equipment, within the first 60 days of approval.
3.1.2 Company-owned laptops and peripherals issued for office use remain Company property and must be returned on request or on separation.

### 3.2 Internet Connectivity
3.2.1 Employees working from home 2 or more days per week are eligible for a monthly internet reimbursement as described in the Reimbursement Policy.

## 4. Core Hours and Availability
4.1 All employees, whether in the office or working from home, are expected to be reachable on chat and video during core hours of 11:00 to 17:00 IST, Monday to Friday.
4.2 Employees working from home must keep their calendar accurate and join scheduled meetings on video with their camera on, unless a manager has agreed otherwise.

## 5. Equipment, Connectivity, and Security
5.1 Employees working from home must maintain a stable internet connection of at least 20 Mbps and a private, distraction-free workspace suitable for confidential calls.
5.2 Company data must only be accessed on Company-issued or Company-approved, encrypted devices. Use of public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks for Company work is prohibited; a Company-issued VPN must be used for all remote access to internal systems.
5.3 Printing confidential documents at home is prohibited unless the employee has a Company-approved secure shredding arrangement in place.

## 6. International Remote Work
6.1 Working remotely from a country other than the employee's designated country of employment for more than 10 working days in a calendar year requires prior written approval from HR and the Legal team, owing to tax, immigration, and data-protection implications.
6.2 Unapproved international remote work may result in loss of company health coverage while abroad and is a disciplinary matter.

## 7. Performance and Attendance
7.1 Remote work is a privilege tied to sustained performance and is not a contractual entitlement. A manager may pause an individual's work-from-home arrangement, with reasons recorded on the HR portal, if performance or availability concerns arise.
7.2 Employees are expected to log actual hours worked accurately regardless of location.

## 8. Policy Review
8.1 This policy is reviewed annually by HR and may be updated to reflect changes in tax, labour law, or business needs. Employees will be notified of material changes at least 30 days in advance.

## 9. Contact
